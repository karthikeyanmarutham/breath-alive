Our story

Why we started BreatheAlive.

Breathing is often treated as a single symptom — a blocked nose, a snore, a habit. But in practice, the airway sits at the centre of a much larger picture: how we sleep, how the face grows, how the body recovers, and how we feel day to day.

BreatheAlive was founded to bring that picture together. Instead of looking at concerns in isolation, we look at how they connect — from nasal patency and oral function to sleep quality and facial development — and build care around the person in front of us.

Today, our clinics in Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Kannur serve infants, children and adults with one consistent standard: specialist-led assessment, clear explanations, and a plan that respects each patient's context.

Our philosophy

How we think about care.

Three principles guide every consultation, every plan, and every follow-up.

Airway-first

We begin with the airway because it influences breathing, sleep, facial growth and overall function. Starting here helps us see the whole picture — not just the loudest symptom.

Whole-person

Breathing is not separate from sleep, posture, feeding, growth or daily energy. We assess how these areas interact — because care that ignores context rarely lasts.

Evidence-informed

Our recommendations follow current clinical understanding, and we are clear about what is established, what is evolving, and what is not yet known. No exaggerated claims — ever.
