1. General information only

The content on the BreatheAlive website — including text, articles, illustrations, FAQs, and other materials — is provided for general information and educational purposes only. It is not intended to be, and should not be relied upon as, medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

2. No doctor-patient relationship

Visiting this website, reading its content, or submitting an enquiry form does not create a doctor-patient relationship. A doctor-patient relationship is established only after an in-person or teleconsultation with a qualified specialist at BreatheAlive and after appropriate consent is provided.

3. No diagnosis or treatment

Information on this site is not a substitute for consultation with a qualified healthcare professional. You should not use the content of this website to:

  • Self-diagnose a medical condition.
  • Delay or avoid seeking professional medical advice.
  • Discontinue or change any treatment prescribed by a qualified clinician.
  • Start any new treatment without professional guidance.

Every patient is different. Assessment findings, recommendations, and treatment plans are specific to each individual and require direct clinical evaluation.

4. Medical emergencies

In case of a medical emergency

This website and any contact forms are not intended for medical emergencies. If you or someone else is experiencing a medical emergency, please contact your local emergency services immediately, or go to the nearest emergency department.

5. Individual results

Any patient stories, testimonials, or outcomes described on this website reflect individual experiences. Individual results may vary depending on the patient's condition, age, medical history, treatment plan, adherence, and other factors. Nothing on this website should be interpreted as a guarantee of any specific outcome.

6. External content

This website may link to external resources, research, or third-party websites. We do not control, endorse, or take responsibility for the accuracy, completeness, or reliability of external content. Links are provided for convenience and reference only.

7. Accuracy & updates

We make reasonable efforts to ensure that the information on this website is accurate and up to date. Medical knowledge evolves continuously, and content may not always reflect the most recent developments. We reserve the right to update, modify, or remove content at any time without notice.

Where clinical content is published, we aim to include author and reviewer information and "last reviewed" dates. If you notice an error or outdated information, please contact us.

8. Always seek professional advice

Always seek the advice of a qualified healthcare professional with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition or treatment. Never disregard professional medical advice, or delay seeking it, because of something you have read on this website.
