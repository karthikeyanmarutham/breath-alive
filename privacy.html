1. Overview

BreatheAlive ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who interacts with our website, clinics, and services. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share personal information when you visit our website, contact us, or book an appointment.

This policy applies to breathealive.in and its subpages, as well as to information collected at our clinics in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Kannur.

2. Information we collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

Information you provide to us

  • Contact details — name, phone number, email address, preferred city.
  • Appointment information — reason for consultation, preferred date and time, patient type (infant, child, adult).
  • Health information — information you voluntarily share about symptoms, concerns, or medical history through forms, emails, or during consultations.
  • Communications — messages sent via our contact form, email, WhatsApp, or phone.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — pages viewed, time on page, and general interaction patterns.
  • Device & browser information — IP address, browser type, operating system, and approximate location derived from IP.
  • Cookies — small files stored on your device. See section 7 for details.

3. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide requested information.
  • Schedule, confirm, and manage appointments.
  • Coordinate care across our clinical team where relevant.
  • Improve our website, services, and patient experience.
  • Send occasional updates you have opted into (e.g. resources newsletter).
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Health information

Health information is treated with the highest level of confidentiality. We only collect health information that is relevant to your consultation or enquiry, and we use it strictly for the purpose of providing care or responding to your request.

Sensitive information: Please do not share detailed medical history through general enquiry forms. We recommend sharing sensitive health information directly during a consultation or via a secure channel we provide.

5. Legal basis for processing

We process personal information on the following bases:

  • Consent — for newsletters and optional communications.
  • Contract — to provide services you have requested, such as scheduling an appointment.
  • Legitimate interest — to improve our services, ensure site security, and communicate with you about your care.
  • Legal obligation — where required by applicable law or regulation.

6. Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Clinical team members — to coordinate your care where appropriate.
  • Service providers — such as hosting, email, or appointment systems, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Referring or coordinating specialists — only with your knowledge when part of your care.
  • Legal authorities — where we are required to do so by law or to protect rights and safety.

7. Cookies & analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, remember preferences, and understand how the site is used.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site features but should not prevent you from accessing core content.

Where analytics tools are used, we configure them to minimise the collection of personally identifiable information.

8. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration. These include encrypted transmission (HTTPS), access controls, and periodic review of our practices.

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We encourage you not to share sensitive information through unsecured channels.

9. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law. Appointment and clinical records are retained in accordance with applicable medical record retention requirements in India. Enquiry data is typically retained for a shorter period.

10. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information, where legally permissible.
  • Withdraw consent for optional communications.
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant authority.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details at the end of this policy.

11. Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended for parents, guardians, and adult patients. For consultations involving infants, children, or adolescents, personal information is provided by a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect information from children without appropriate consent.

12. Third-party services

Our website may include links to third-party services, including Google Maps for location information and WhatsApp for messaging. These services operate under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of our website after any changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
