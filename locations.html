FAQ

Visiting our clinics

Which clinic should I choose?

Choose the clinic closest to you, or the one where your preferred specialist practices. If you're unsure, contact us and we'll guide you.

Are the services the same at every location?

Yes. Infants, children and adult care is available at all three locations, following the same standard of specialist-led assessment.

Do you offer teleconsultation?

Teleconsultation availability depends on your location and concern. Please contact us to check.

Do I need to book in advance?

We recommend booking in advance so we can give you the time and attention you deserve. Please call to check same-day availability.

Is parking available?

Parking and accessibility details vary by location. Please contact the specific clinic for details.

How do I reschedule?

Please call or WhatsApp the clinic you originally booked with. We'll do our best to find a time that works.
