FAQ

Contact questions

How quickly will you reply?

We typically respond within one working day. If your enquiry is urgent, please call us directly.

Can I book an appointment by phone?

Yes. You can call us during clinic hours, or use the Book Appointment form online.

Do you offer teleconsultation?

Teleconsultation availability depends on your location and concern. Please contact us to check.

Which clinic should I choose?

Choose the clinic closest to you, or the one where your preferred specialist practices. If unsure, contact us and we'll guide you.

Do you accept walk-ins?

We recommend booking in advance so we can give you the time and attention you deserve. Please call to check same-day availability.
