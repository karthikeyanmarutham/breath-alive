Gentle, specialist-led assessment for infants — helping parents understand breathing, feeding and airway-related concerns early.

What we look at

A gentle, whole-picture assessment.

Breathing patterns

How your baby breathes at rest and during sleep.

Feeding & latch

Comfort, efficiency and coordination during feeds.

Oral function

Tongue, lip and jaw movement relevant to feeding and airway.

Tongue-tie assessment

Where clinically appropriate, and with referral as needed.

Sleep & settling

Patterns that may be relevant to breathing and rest.

Airway development

How early function may influence longer-term growth.

Signs to watch

When parents often reach out.

  • Noisy breathing
  • Difficult or uncomfortable feeding
  • Frequent waking or unsettled sleep
  • Mouth breathing at rest
  • Slow weight gain
  • Reflux-like symptoms

This is not a diagnosis. Please consult a qualified specialist for advice specific to your child.

FAQ

Questions parents ask

When should I bring my baby?

If you notice persistent breathing, feeding or sleep concerns, an early consultation can help. Please consult a specialist for advice specific to your baby.

Is the assessment painful?

Assessments are gentle and infant-appropriate. We explain everything before we begin.

Do you treat tongue-tie?

We assess oral function including tongue-tie where clinically appropriate, and coordinate referral when needed.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly. Please contact us if you have questions.
